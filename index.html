Touch Typing System – Basics

The touch typing system is based on the **Home Row Position**. Your index fingers rest on the keys **F** and **J** (the small bump helps you find them blindly).

Left Hand

  • • Pinky: A, Q, Y
  • • Ring: S, W
  • • Middle: D, E
  • • Index: F, R, G, T, V, C
  • • Thumb: Spacebar

Right Hand

  • • Index: J, U, H, Z, B, N
  • • Middle: K, I
  • • Ring: L, O
  • • Pinky: P, Ö, Ä, Ü
  • • Thumb: Spacebar
